# Total school ranking addendum

See [the approved decision](../decisions/2026-09-21-total-school-ranking.md).

Home presents a standalone `총 학교 순위` box immediately after the hero. It reuses the approved siege video and shows the five highest schools by published cumulative XP. The first three are visually dominant crowned podiums and each displays its XP; fourth and fifth are compact challenger rows with supportive copy and XP.

The public ranking and school-hub rank use the same cumulative, privacy-batched projection. Weekly labels and recent-seven-day ranking language are removed from this flow. Empty and unavailable states remain honest; no example schools or scores are shown in production.

Anonymous school notes are excluded from this addendum.
