# Approved Game Visual Experience V1

The 2026-09-10 user runbook authorizes the AFTER SCHOOL / OUR SCHOOL WORLD visual
contract recorded in `../decisions/2026-09-10-game-visual-experience-v1.md`.

Home leads with school growth, search and a decorative floating campus. Its guest
panel makes no numerical claim; authenticated owner cards use existing owner data.
Empty ranking stays empty. Published ranking and milestones use public aggregates.
Hub uses the common campus world, actual level/progress and existing entry action.
My Schools brings live growth ahead of account management. Share keeps a separate
decorative world with no owner level/progress and the existing explicit preparation
flow. Search, Google login, registration intent and account controls remain intact.

Five deterministic frontend stages at levels 1 / 2–3 / 4–6 / 7–9 / 10+ change
illustration, scale, ambient accents and aura only. They unlock no features.
Two approved transparent campus illustrations form the asset system. Levels 1–3
share the smaller campus with differing scale/warmth; higher stages share a larger
campus with differing aura/stars. Separate per-stage buildings are not claimed.

Navy/pastel tokens are scoped to game surfaces. Admin and safety forms retain
existing semantics. Motion stops for reduced-motion preferences. Navigation keeps
Home, school search, connections and account access; no fake events/reward claims.

No database, migration, permission, environment, XP or referral-rule changes.
Preview is the release gate; Production requires subsequent user visual approval.
