# Approved Fantasy V3

See [decision](../decisions/2026-09-15-fantasy-v3-application.md). V3 is the approved visual treatment for home, school search, login, onboarding, account school cards, school pages, connections, welcome guidance and school sharing.

Full-frame fantasy artwork, violet panels and gold primary actions replace the realistic contextual treatment. Search remains available through the home school-finding action and the dedicated search screen. Home is a reunion introduction rather than a search-field landing page. Decorative motion must be pausable and respect reduced-motion and visibility settings. Functional and privacy contracts are unchanged.

The subsequent approved home-light refinement aligns warm light pulses, cyan waterfall glints and gold flag highlights to the source artwork. All effects share its responsive crop and the existing motion controls. See `docs/decisions/2026-09-15-home-light-shimmer.md`.

The user-approved 2026-09-16 clarity refinement reduces the purple wash over the artwork, reserves a clear mobile character area, and serves the hero at a resolution matching its cover plane. The connection hint is one short line before the actions: “안부를 보내고, 서로 수락하면 연결돼요.” The image, logo and functional contracts are preserved. See [clarity decision](../decisions/2026-09-16-home-hero-clarity.md).
