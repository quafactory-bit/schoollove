# Approved Fantasy V3

See [decision](../decisions/2026-09-15-fantasy-v3-application.md). V3 is the approved visual treatment for home, school search, login, onboarding, account school cards, school pages, connections, welcome guidance and school sharing.

Full-frame fantasy artwork, violet panels and gold primary actions replace the realistic contextual treatment. Search remains available through the home school-finding action and the dedicated search screen. Home is a reunion introduction rather than a search-field landing page. Decorative motion must be pausable and respect reduced-motion and visibility settings. Functional and privacy contracts are unchanged.
